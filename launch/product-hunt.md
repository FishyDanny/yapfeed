# Product Hunt draft

## Name

Yapfeed

## Tagline

Short public-domain audio that continues while your eyes rest

## Description

Press play once for a continuous queue of short, credited LibriVox recordings streamed from Internet Archive. Skip with one large control or the Space key, set a sleep timer, and listen without creating an account.

## First comment

The idea came from my partner: short entertainment for people who want to rest their eyes and do not want to commit to hours of podcast listening.

I started with the listening experience rather than a social network. Yapfeed has 58 seeded public-domain recordings, auto-advance, a large next control, a keyboard shortcut, a sleep timer and Media Session actions where supported. Each piece displays its author, LibriVox collection, source and public-domain status.

There are no profiles, follows, comments or automatic publishing. Submitted audio links go to a private human-review queue. Listening needs no account; likes and skips stay in the browser. Anonymous completed/skipped events are recorded to assess whether the feed is worth continuing.

Physical locked-phone testing is still incomplete, so browser and operating-system background behaviour is a known question. I would appreciate device-specific reports and feedback on whether the current mix sustains a short listening session.
