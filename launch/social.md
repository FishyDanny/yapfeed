# Social drafts

These are drafts for a human to post. Check the live app first and do not describe screen-off playback as verified until it has passed a physical locked-phone test.

## The problem

My partner wanted short entertainment without keeping her eyes on a screen or committing to a full podcast episode. I built the listener part: press play once and short, credited public-domain stories continue automatically. https://s72-yapfeed.pages.dev

## What is in the feed

Yapfeed starts with 58 LibriVox volunteer recordings from five public-domain Internet Archive collections. Every piece shows its author, collection, source and licence status. The audio stays with its source; Yapfeed streams it rather than hosting a copy. https://s72-yapfeed.pages.dev
